# Statement of Complainant (Neha Gupta)

I purchased a ProBook laptop on 25 April 2024 billed as 'Brand New Sealed Retail Unit' for ₹85,000. When I registered the device for manufacturer support, the official portal confirmed the warranty was activated on 10 October 2023 by an earlier customer. The seller supplied a repackaged refurbished unit. I claim refund of ₹85,000 plus ₹25,000 for unfair trade practice.
