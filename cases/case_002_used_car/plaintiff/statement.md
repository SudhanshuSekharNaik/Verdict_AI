# Statement of Complainant (Priya Sharma)

I purchased a 2021 sedan from Rajesh Auto Dealership on 20 March 2024 for ₹6,50,000 relying on their written declaration that the vehicle was 100% accident free. In May 2024, an authorised inspection detected extensive chassis straightening and welded joints from a November 2023 crash. I demand full cancellation, refund of ₹6,50,000, and compensation for fraudulent misrepresentation.
