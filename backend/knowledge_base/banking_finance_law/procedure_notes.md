# Procedural Practice Notes — Banking & SARFAESI Disputes

### Cheque Dishonour Timelines (§138 NI Act)
1. Cheque presentation within 3 months of date.
2. Statutory demand notice dispatched within 30 days of receiving bank memo.
3. 15-day grace payment period for drawer.
4. Complaint filed before Judicial Magistrate within 30 days of expiry of 15-day grace period.

### DRT Pre-Deposit & SARFAESI Remedies
1. Section 18 SARFAESI appeal to DRAT requires mandatory minimum 25% to 50% pre-deposit of debt amount.
2. DM / CMM assistance under Section 14 for taking physical possession requires affidavit verifying compliance with Section 13(2) notice.
