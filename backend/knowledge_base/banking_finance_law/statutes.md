# Banking, Debt Recovery & Negotiable Instruments Statutes

### Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881 (NI Act)
- **Section 138:** Dishonour of cheque for insufficiency of funds in the account punishable with imprisonment up to 2 years or fine up to twice the cheque amount.
- **Section 139 (Statutory Presumption):** Rebuttable presumption that holder of a cheque received the cheque for the discharge, in whole or in part, of any debt or other liability.
- **Section 141:** Offences by companies; directors in charge of and responsible for company conduct vicariously liable.

### SARFAESI Act, 2002
- **Section 13(2):** 60-day demand notice issued by secured creditor to borrower upon classification of account as Non-Performing Asset (NPA).
- **Section 13(4):** Measures for enforcement of security interest (taking physical/symbolic possession, taking over management).
- **Section 17:** Application before Debt Recovery Tribunal (DRT) by borrower challenging SARFAESI measures.
