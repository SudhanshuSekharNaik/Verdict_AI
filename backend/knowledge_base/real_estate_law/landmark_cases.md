# Landmark Precedents in Real Estate Law

### Imperia Structures Ltd. v. Anil Patni, (2020) 10 SCC 783
**Bench & Court:** Supreme Court of India (U.U. Lalit, J.)
**Core Holding:** Held that remedies available to allottees/homebuyers under the Consumer Protection Act and RERA are concurrent and mutually complementary. Allottees are not barred from approaching consumer fora even if RERA applies.

### Pioneer Urban Land & Infrastructure Ltd. v. Govindan Raghavan, (2019) 5 SCC 725
**Bench & Court:** Supreme Court of India
**Core Holding:** One-sided standard clauses in builder agreements favoring the developer constitute unfair trade practices; an allottee cannot be compelled to accept delayed possession.
