# Real Estate & Property Law Statutes

### Transfer of Property Act, 1882 (TPA)
- **Section 53A (Part Performance):** Protection of transferee who has taken possession in furtherance of contract and performed their obligations.
- **Section 54 (Sale of Immovable Property):** Transfer of ownership in exchange for a price paid or promised; requires registered instrument for value over ₹100.
- **Section 58 (Mortgages):** Transfer of interest in specific immovable property for securing payment of money advanced.

### Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 (RERA)
- **Section 14 & 18:** Strict promoter liability for failure to deliver possession within stipulated timeline; homebuyer entitled to refund with interest or monthly delay compensation.
