# Landmark Precedents in Corporate & Commercial Law

### Tata Consultancy Services Ltd. v. Cyrus Investments Pvt. Ltd., (2021) 9 SCC 449
**Bench & Court:** Supreme Court of India (S.A. Bobde, CJI)
**Core Holding:** Clarified the high threshold for establishing oppression and mismanagement under Sections 241/242 of Companies Act. Removal of a Director by majority shareholders per Articles of Association does not automatically amount to oppression unless lack of probity or fraud is demonstrated.

### Kailash Nath Associates v. DDA, (2015) 4 SCC 136
**Bench & Court:** Supreme Court of India (R.F. Nariman, J.)
**Core Holding:** Section 74 of the Contract Act requires proof of actual damage or loss before earnest money or liquidated damages can be forfeited, unless it is impossible to prove damages.
