# Corporate & Commercial Law Statutes

### Companies Act, 2013
- **Section 166:** Fiduciary duties of directors to act in good faith to promote the objects of the company for the benefit of members and avoid conflicts of interest.
- **Section 241 & 242:** Application to NCLT for relief in cases of oppression of minority shareholders and mismanagement.
- **Section 135:** Mandatory Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) compliance for qualifying companies.

### Indian Contract Act, 1872
- **Section 10:** Essential elements of valid contracts (free consent, competent parties, lawful consideration, lawful object).
- **Section 73:** Compensation for loss or damage caused by breach of contract (naturally arising in the usual course or known to parties).
- **Section 74:** Liquidated damages and penalty clauses (reasonable compensation not exceeding the amount named).
