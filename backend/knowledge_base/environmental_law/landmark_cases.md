# Landmark Precedents in Environmental Law

### Vellore Citizens' Welfare Forum v. Union of India, (1996) 5 SCC 647
**Bench & Court:** Supreme Court of India (Kuldip Singh, J.)
**Core Holding:** Integrated the "Precautionary Principle" and the "Polluter Pays Principle" as essential features of sustainable development and part of the environmental law of the land under Article 21.

### M.C. Mehta v. Union of India (Oleum Gas Leak), (1987) 1 SCC 395
**Bench & Court:** Supreme Court of India (P.N. Bhagwati, CJI)
**Core Holding:** Propounded the doctrine of "Absolute Liability" for enterprises engaged in hazardous or inherently dangerous activities, rejecting exceptions to strict liability under Rylands v. Fletcher.
