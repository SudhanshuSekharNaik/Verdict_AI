# Procedural Practice Notes — NGT Litigation

### Limitation & Expert Committee Procedure
1. **Limitation under Section 14 NGT Act:** 6 months from the date on which the cause of action first arose, extendable by max 60 days on sufficient cause.
2. **Joint Expert Committees:** Standard practice of NGT appointing multi-disciplinary expert committees (CPCB, SPCB, MoEFCC) for ground-truthing environmental damage.
