# Environmental Law Statutes

### Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 (EPA)
- **Section 3 & 5:** Powers of Central Government to take measures for protecting and improving the environment and issue binding closure/stoppage directions.
- **EIA Notification 2006:** Mandatory prior environmental clearance for category A and B industrial and infrastructure projects.

### National Green Tribunal Act, 2010 (NGT Act)
- **Section 14 & 15:** Jurisdiction over civil environmental cases arising out of implementation of enactments; power to grant relief, compensation, and restitution of damaged property and environment.
- **Section 20:** Mandatory application of Sustainable Development, Precautionary Principle, and Polluter Pays Principle by the Tribunal.
