# Landmark Precedents in Cyber Law

### Shreya Singhal v. Union of India, (2015) 5 SCC 1
**Bench & Court:** Supreme Court of India (R.F. Nariman, J.)
**Core Holding:** Struck down Section 66A of the IT Act as unconstitutional for violating freedom of speech under Article 19(1)(a). Clarified Section 79 intermediary safe harbour: an intermediary must take down unlawful content only upon receiving actual knowledge via a court order or authorized government notification.

### Anvar P.V. v. P.K. Basheer, (2014) 10 SCC 473
**Bench & Court:** Supreme Court of India (Kurian Joseph, J.)
**Core Holding:** Held that electronic records are inadmissible as secondary evidence unless accompanied by the mandatory statutory certificate. Oral evidence cannot substitute the certificate.

### Arjun Panditrao Khotkar v. Kailash Kushanrao Gorantyal, (2020) 7 SCC 1
**Bench & Court:** Supreme Court of India (R.F. Nariman, J.)
**Core Holding:** Confirmed that producing a certificate is mandatory when secondary electronic evidence is tendered, but clarified that trial courts may direct production of the certificate where an official or entity refuses to furnish it.
