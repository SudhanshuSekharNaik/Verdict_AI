# Procedural Practice Notes — Cyber Law

### Electronic Seizure & Hash Integrity Protocol
1. **Digital Evidence Extraction:** Devices seized must be placed in Faraday bags, with SHA-256 or MD5 hash values generated immediately at the scene to prove no post-seizure tampering.
2. **Intermediary Notice Compliance:** Notice under Rule 3 of the IT (Intermediary Guidelines) Rules requires 36-hour takedown response window upon receipt of valid judicial order.
