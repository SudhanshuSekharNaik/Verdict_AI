# Cyber Law & IT Offences Statutes

### Information Technology Act, 2000 (IT Act)
- **Section 43:** Penalty for damage to computer systems, unauthorized downloading, copying, virus injection, or denial of access.
- **Section 66:** Computer-related offences committed dishonestly or fraudulently punishable with imprisonment up to 3 years or fine up to ₹5 lakh.
- **Section 66C & 66D:** Identity theft using passwords/electronic signatures, and cheating by personation using computer resource.
- **Section 66E & 67:** Violation of bodily privacy and publishing or transmitting obscene material in electronic form.
- **Section 69A:** Power of Central Government to issue blocking directions for public access to computer information in the interest of sovereignty and public order.
- **Section 79 (Safe Harbour):** Intermediary liability exemption where the intermediary does not initiate transmission, select receiver, or modify content, subject to due diligence.

### Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 Section 61 & 63 — Electronic Evidence
Mandatory electronic certificate verifying the system was operating properly, identifying the hash value, and establishing continuous chain of custody.
