# Landmark Precedents in Tax Law

### Vodafone International Holdings B.V. v. Union of India, (2012) 6 SCC 613
**Bench & Court:** Supreme Court of India (S.H. Kapadia, CJI)
**Core Holding:** Established the "look at" rather than "look through" approach to genuine corporate share transfer transactions involving foreign holding structures, emphasizing certainty in fiscal statutes.

### Union of India v. Mohit Minerals Pvt. Ltd., (2022) 10 SCC 700
**Bench & Court:** Supreme Court of India (D.Y. Chandrachud, J.)
**Core Holding:** Clarified the advisory nature of GST Council recommendations and held that separate levy of IGST on ocean freight in CIF contracts is impermissible where customs value already covers freight.
