# Taxation & Revenue Law Statutes

### Income Tax Act, 1961
- **Section 147 & 148:** Reassessment of income escaping assessment; requires tangible material and live link before issuing reassessment notice.
- **Section 37:** General deduction for expenditure laid out wholly and exclusively for the purpose of business or profession.

### Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 (CGST Act)
- **Section 16:** Eligibility and conditions for taking Input Tax Credit (ITC) on invoice issuance and tax payment.
- **Section 73 & 74:** Determination of tax unpaid or short paid for reasons other than fraud (Section 73) or by reason of fraud/willful misstatement (Section 74).
