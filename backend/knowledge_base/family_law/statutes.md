# Family & Matrimonial Law Statutes

### Hindu Marriage Act, 1955 (HMA) Section 13 — Divorce Grounds
(1) Any marriage solemnized under this Act may be dissolved by a decree of divorce on a petition presented by either the husband or the wife on the grounds that the other party:
- (ia) has, after the solemnization of the marriage, treated the petitioner with cruelty (physical or mental);
- (ib) has deserted the petitioner for a continuous period of not less than two years immediately preceding the presentation of the petition;
- (i) has, after the solemnization of the marriage, had voluntary sexual intercourse with any person other than his or her spouse.

### Hindu Marriage Act, 1955 Section 13B — Divorce by Mutual Consent
(1) A petition for dissolution of marriage by a decree of divorce may be presented to the district court by both the parties to a marriage together, on the ground that they have been living separately for a period of one year or more, that they have not been able to live together and that they have mutually agreed that the marriage should be dissolved.

### Hindu Marriage Act, 1955 Section 24 & 25 — Maintenance Pendente Lite and Permanent Alimony
- Section 24 provides interim maintenance and expenses of proceedings where either spouse has no independent income sufficient for their support.
- Section 25 empowers the court to award gross sum or monthly maintenance having regard to the respondent's income, property, and conduct of the parties.

### Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005 (PWDVA)
- **Section 3:** Definition of domestic violence including physical, sexual, verbal, emotional, and economic abuse.
- **Section 12:** Application to Magistrate for protection orders, residence orders, and monetary relief.
- **Section 19:** Residence orders protecting the aggrieved woman's right to reside in the shared household regardless of legal title.
- **Section 20:** Monetary reliefs including medical expenses, loss of earnings, and maintenance for the aggrieved woman and her children.

### Special Marriage Act, 1954 (SMA) Section 27 — Divorce
Provides secular grounds for divorce including adultery, desertion for two years, imprisonment for seven years, and cruelty for marriages registered under the Special Marriage Act.

### Guardians and Wards Act, 1890 Section 7 & 17 — Minor's Custody
Section 17 mandates that in appointing or declaring the guardian of a minor, the Court shall be guided by what appears to be for the welfare of the minor, considering age, sex, religion, and the wishes of a capable minor.
