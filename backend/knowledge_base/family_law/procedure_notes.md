# Procedural Practice Notes — Family Law & Domestic Adjudication

### Standard of Proof & Evidentiary Thresholds
1. **Preponderance of Probabilities:** Unlike criminal trials, matrimonial disputes under the Hindu Marriage Act and Special Marriage Act are governed by the civil standard of preponderance of probabilities.
2. **Standard on Cruelty & Desertion:** Requires cogent circumstantial or documentary corroboration (medical records, correspondence, proof of independent residence, police complaints) demonstrating an intolerable matrimonial atmosphere.

### Mandatory Pre-Trial Mediation & Reconciliation
1. Under Section 9 of the Family Courts Act 1984 and HMA Section 23(2), the Family Court is statutorily obligated to endeavor to bring about reconciliation between the spouses before proceeding to contentious trial.
2. If mediation fails, the court frames specific legal issues regarding cruelty, desertion, quantum of maintenance, and child custody.

### Interim Protections & Affidavits of Disclosure
1. Applications under Section 24 HMA or Section 12 DV Act require filing of Rajnesh v. Neha asset disclosure affidavits within 4 weeks.
2. Ex-parte interim protection orders under Section 23 DV Act may be granted upon prima facie showing of imminent domestic violence or economic dispossession.
