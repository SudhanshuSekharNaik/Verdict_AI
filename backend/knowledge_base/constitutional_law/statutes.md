# Constitutional Law Statutes & Provisions

### Constitution of India — Fundamental Rights (Part III)
- **Article 14:** Equality before the law and equal protection of the laws; prohibition of arbitrariness in State action.
- **Article 19(1)(a)-(g):** Fundamental freedoms (speech, peaceful assembly, association, movement, residence, profession) subject to reasonable restrictions under Art 19(2)-(6).
- **Article 21:** Protection of life and personal liberty; right to fair trial, bodily integrity, dignified livelihood, and privacy.
- **Article 32 & 226:** Constitutional remedies — Power of Supreme Court and High Courts to issue writs of Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Prohibition, Quo Warranto, and Certiorari for enforcement of fundamental and legal rights.
