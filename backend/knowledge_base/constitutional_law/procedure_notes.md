# Procedural Practice Notes — Constitutional Writ Jurisdiction

### Scope of Judicial Review & Locus Standi
1. **Writ Guidelines:** Writs lie against "State" under Article 12 or bodies discharging public functions.
2. **Proportionality Doctrine:** State restrictions on fundamental rights must satisfy the 4-prong proportionality test (legitimate aim, suitability, necessity, and strict balancing).
