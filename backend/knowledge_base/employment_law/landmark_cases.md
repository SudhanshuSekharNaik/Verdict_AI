# Landmark Precedents in Employment Law

### Bangalore Water Supply and Sewerage Board v. A. Rajappa, (1978) 2 SCC 213
**Bench & Court:** 7-Judge Constitution Bench (Krishna Iyer, J.)
**Core Holding:** Established the comprehensive "Triple Test" to define "industry" under Section 2(j) IDA: systematic activity, cooperation between employer and employee, for production/distribution of goods and services.

### State of Karnataka v. Umadevi (3), (2006) 4 SCC 1
**Bench & Court:** 5-Judge Constitution Bench
**Core Holding:** Held that public employment must adhere to Article 14 and 16; contractual or temporary employees cannot claim automatic regularization unless appointed against sanctioned posts through regular selection.
