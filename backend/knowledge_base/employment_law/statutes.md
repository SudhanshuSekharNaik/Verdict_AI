# Labour & Employment Law Statutes

### Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 (IDA)
- **Section 2(oo) & 25F:** Definition of retrenchment and conditions precedent for retrenchment (1 month notice and 15 days average pay per completed year of continuous service).
- **Section 33C(2):** Recovery of money or statutory benefits due from employer through Labour Court adjudication.

### Code on Wages, 2019 & POSH Act, 2013
- Universal statutory entitlement to minimum wages, timely wage payment, and equal remuneration.
- Prevention of Sexual Harassment at Workplace (POSH Act) Section 4 & 11: Mandatory Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) and fair domestic enquiry.
