# Procedural Practice Notes — Criminal Law & Trial Conduct

### Standard of Proof & Presumption of Innocence
1. **Unshifting Burden (BSA §104):** The prosecution bears the burden of establishing every material element of the charge beyond all reasonable doubt. The accused is clothed with a constitutional presumption of innocence until the final judicial holding.
2. **Standard on Exceptions (BSA §108):** When an accused invokes an affirmative defense (such as Exception 1 to BNS §103 for provocation or private defense), the standard of proof placed on the defense is preponderance of probabilities, not beyond reasonable doubt.

### Stage-Wise Examination Protocol under BNSS
1. **Examination-in-Chief:** Conducted by the party calling the witness to elicit material facts relevant to the indictment. No leading questions are permitted without the leave of the court.
2. **Cross-Examination:** Opposing counsel tests credibility, veracity, sensory vantage point, memory, and challenges inconsistencies with prior police statements. Leading questions are permitted.
3. **Re-Examination:** Confined strictly to explaining matters referred to in cross-examination; no new matters may be introduced without judicial permission.

### Electronic Record Admissibility under BSA §61 / §63
For CCTV footage, call detail records (CDR), or computer logs:
- Must be accompanied by an electronic certificate identifying the device, verifying lawful control and uninterrupted integrity.
- Absence of a valid hash or chain of custody creates serious evidentiary gaps regarding tampering or spoliation.
