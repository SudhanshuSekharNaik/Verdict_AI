# Procedural Practice Notes — Civil Litigation

### Burden of Proof & Pleading Rules
1. **Burden on Plaintiff:** Plaintiff must prove their affirmative claims on a balance of probabilities (Section 101/102 Evidence Act / BSA §104).
2. **Strict Adherence to Pleadings (Order 6 Rule 2):** Parties cannot lead evidence on facts not pleaded in the Plaint or Written Statement.
3. **Execution of Decrees (Order 21):** Money decrees and recovery of possession executed through attachment, sale, or civil detention.
