# Civil Law & Procedure Statutes

### Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 (CPC)
- **Section 9:** Courts have jurisdiction to try all suits of a civil nature excepting suits of which their cognizance is either expressly or impliedly barred.
- **Section 11 (Res Judicata):** No Court shall try any suit or issue in which the matter directly and substantially in issue has been heard and finally decided by a Court of competent jurisdiction.
- **Order 39 Rules 1 & 2 (Temporary Injunctions):** Grounded on the tripartite test: (1) Prima facie case; (2) Balance of convenience; (3) Irreparable injury that cannot be adequately compensated in monetary damages.
- **Order 7 Rule 11 (Rejection of Plaint):** Plaint rejected where it does not disclose a cause of action, is barred by any law (e.g. Limitation Act), or is insufficiently stamped.

### Specific Relief Act, 1963 (SRA)
- **Section 10 (Specific Performance):** Specific performance of a contract shall be enforced by the court subject to statutory exceptions, making specific enforcement mandatory rather than discretionary following the 2018 amendment.
- **Section 14 (Contracts Not Specifically Enforceable):** Contracts involving personal skills or continuous supervision cannot be specifically enforced.
- **Section 34 (Declaratory Decrees):** Court may make a declaration of legal character or right where plaintiff is entitled to any legal character or property right.
- **Section 38 (Perpetual Injunctions):** Granted to prevent the breach of an obligation existing in favor of the plaintiff.

### Indian Limitation Act, 1963
- Suits for breach of contract, recovery of money, or specific performance have a general limitation period of 3 years from the date of cause of action.
