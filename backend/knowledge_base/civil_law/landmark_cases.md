# Landmark Precedents in Indian Civil Law

### Gujarat Bottling Co. Ltd. v. Coca Cola Co., (1995) 5 SCC 545
**Bench & Court:** Supreme Court of India
**Core Holding:** Settled the cardinal principles governing grant of interlocutory injunctions under CPC Order 39. The court must assess prima facie case, balance of convenience, and whether refusing injunction results in irreparable loss. Equitable relief is denied to parties with unclean hands.

### Morgan Stanley Mutual Fund v. Kartick Das, (1994) 4 SCC 225
**Bench & Court:** Supreme Court of India
**Core Holding:** Laid down strict parameters for ex-parte ad-interim injunctions: they should be granted only in exceptional circumstances where delay would defeat justice, with notice to the opposing party being the general rule.

### Surya Dev Rai v. Ram Chander Rai, (2003) 6 SCC 675
**Bench & Court:** Supreme Court of India
**Core Holding:** Clarified the scope of supervisory jurisdiction under Article 227 of the Constitution and Section 115 CPC revisions against interlocutory orders of civil courts.
