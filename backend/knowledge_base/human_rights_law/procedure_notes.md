# Procedural Practice Notes — Human Rights Inquiries

### NHRC Inquiries & Custodial Death Guidelines
1. **Reporting Within 24 Hours:** Mandatory reporting of any custodial death to NHRC within 24 hours followed by video-recorded post-mortem and magisterial inquest under BNSS §196.
2. **Standard of Compensation:** State compensation awarded ex debito justitiae without waiting for completion of criminal trial against errant police officers.
