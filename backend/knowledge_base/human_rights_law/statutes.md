# Human Rights & Civil Liberties Statutes

### Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993 (PHRA)
- **Section 12:** Functions of the National Human Rights Commission (NHRC) to inquire into human rights violations by public servants and intervene in court proceedings involving allegations of human rights abuse.
- **Section 18:** Recommendations for interim compensation and prosecution of guilty state officials.

### Constitutional Protections (Articles 20, 21, 22)
- **Article 20(3):** Protection against self-incrimination.
- **Article 22:** Mandatory communication of grounds of arrest, right to consult counsel, and production before Magistrate within 24 hours.
