# Landmark Precedents in Human Rights Jurisprudence

### D.K. Basu v. State of West Bengal, (1997) 1 SCC 416
**Bench & Court:** Supreme Court of India (A.S. Anand, J.)
**Core Holding:** Formulated 11 mandatory guidelines for police personnel carrying out arrest and detention, including visible identification badges, arrest memo witnessed by family/friend, medical examination every 48 hours, and notification to police control room.

### Nilabati Behera v. State of Orissa, (1993) 2 SCC 746
**Bench & Court:** Supreme Court of India (J.S. Verma, J.)
**Core Holding:** Established public law damages as an independent strict liability remedy under Article 32/226 for violations of fundamental rights by state authorities (custodial death), distinct from private tort remedies.
