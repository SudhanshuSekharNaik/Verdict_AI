# Landmark Precedents in IP Law

### Cadila Health Care Ltd. v. Cadila Pharmaceuticals Ltd., (2001) 5 SCC 73
**Bench & Court:** Supreme Court of India
**Core Holding:** Established the test for deceptive similarity in pharmaceutical products: higher standard of care applies to avoid confusion where medicinal products with similar phonetic names could cause harm.

### Eastern Book Company v. D.B. Modak, (2008) 1 SCC 1
**Bench & Court:** Supreme Court of India
**Core Holding:** Adopted the "modicum of creativity" standard for copyright originality in legal reports and headnotes, rejecting the pure "sweat of the brow" doctrine.
