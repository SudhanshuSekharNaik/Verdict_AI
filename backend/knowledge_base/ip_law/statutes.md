# Intellectual Property Law Statutes

### Trade Marks Act, 1999
- **Section 29:** Infringement of registered trademarks by identical or deceptively similar marks used in the course of trade causing public confusion.
- **Section 27(2):** Common law action for passing off to protect goodwill and business reputation against misrepresentation.

### Copyright Act, 1957
- **Section 14 & 51:** Exclusive rights in literary, dramatic, musical, artistic works and cinematograph films; infringement by unauthorized reproduction, performance, or broadcast.
- **Section 52:** Fair dealing exceptions for private study, research, criticism, review, and reporting of current events.
